  ## Timeline Editor is now available.

  Open Timeline Editor from **Create** tools, or from the top of **Library > Folder**.

  - Place video, audio, images, and more on a timeline and edit them.
  - Create and apply various sound effects that fit your video.
  - Import music you created and use it as background music.
  - Adjust timing and volume per audio track. This enables lip sync.
  - Export your edits as a video.

<Update label="03/6/2026">
  ## Folder feature

  <Frame>
    ![Image](/images/image-21.png)
  </Frame>
  Folders are now available in the Library.

  - Create folders in **Library** to organize your content.
  - When generating images, videos, or music, you can assign a folder for the output.
