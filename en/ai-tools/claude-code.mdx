These are the apps included by default in Daven. You can also add apps created by users.

1. Image app
2. Video app
3. Music app
4. YouTube Summary app
5. Kids Shorts app
6. Kids Storybook app
7. Others

- All creations made in Daven can be imported in the ReadyAction app.
- All creations made in the ReadyAction app are managed in Daven.
