---
title: "Content privacy"
---

How to make your content private. Content privacy is available from the Lite membership tier.

## From the Library

1. Find the content in the Library.
2. Click the **...** on the right of that content.
3. Choose **Public** or **Private**.

## From Settings

1. In Settings, find **Default content visibility**.
2. Set the default visibility for newly created content.
