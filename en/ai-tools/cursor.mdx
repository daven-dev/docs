---
title: "How to create through conversation"
description: "How to create the content you want through conversation. You and Daven AI exchange questions and answers to generate the content you want. "
---

1. Click **Chat** in the left menu.
2. Select **Creator mode** on the left of the bottom box that says "Ask anything."
3. Choose the type of content you want to create by selecting a tool on the right of Creator mode.
4. Generate content through conversation.
5. You can view generated content in the Library.
