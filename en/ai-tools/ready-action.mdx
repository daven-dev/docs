---
title: "ReadyAction app"
---

ReadyAction is a standalone app built on Daven AI. It is designed to make professional video content easily.

1. **Action Agent**: On the [ReadyAction.ai](http://ReadyAction.ai) main page, enter what you want to create in the Action Agent and it will suggest the right generation mode and settings.
2. **Choose from many generation modes**: You can also pick a generation mode from the categories at the top and start from there—e.g. Film, Music Video, Promo, various short-form video formats.
3. **Generation style**: ReadyAction supports fully automated generation via the agent, semi-automatic with your input, and manual creation from scratch. You can decide as you go when you use each option.
4. **Characters**: Characters created in ReadyAction are compatible with Daven.
5. **Tokens**: ReadyAction and Daven share the same token system.
