## Daven AI

English Introduction for Daven AI

## What makes it different

Chat-based AI models or general-purpose AI agents struggle to produce content like movies, short-form videos, or news and cartoons that are delivered automatically on different topics every hour. Traditional approaches generate each step in isolation, so users must manually choose and combine them through endless trial and error. Or an AI agent generates the entire pipeline unilaterally, and users still don't get the result they wanted despite paying time and cost. Daven AI manages multiple AI models through the Daven Orchestration System, guiding creators through each step of creation so they can build what they want. You can then share and distribute in the way you prefer, or connect to YouTube, TikTok, etc., to distribute content automatically.

## What you can do

You can create through chat as the agent runs, or through the apps provided by Daven AI. In particular, we offer an ecosystem where you can launch and serve your own apps on Daven AI.
