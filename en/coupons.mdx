---
title: "Coupons Type"
---

There are token coupons and membership coupons. You can apply coupons after logging in.

## Token coupons

Token coupons let you receive tokens while keeping your current membership.

**How to apply**

- Go to [Settings > Membership plan](https://daven.ai/settings?tabs=membership).
- Click the "Have a coupon?" link on the right of the title.
- Enter the coupon code you received.

## Membership coupons

Membership coupons give you access to membership provided by Daven.

**How to apply**

- Go to the [plan selection page](https://daven.ai/pricing).
- Select the plan that matches your membership coupon and click **Upgrade**\
  e.g. For a $40 Plus coupon, select Plus plan > $40 and click **Upgrade**.
- On the payment page, click **Add promotion code** in the left panel.
- Enter the membership coupon code you received.

<Note>
  Membership coupons are limited-time one-month free promotions. After one month, billing continues automatically. To avoid automatic billing, downgrade to the free plan within one month after applying the coupon: Settings > Membership plan > Manage > Downgrade to free.
</Note>
