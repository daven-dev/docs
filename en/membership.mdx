## Membership plans

Daven AI offers a variety of membership plans.

- **Lite plan**: Some features have usage limits, but you can use many features.
- **Plus plan**: All features are included; generation priority and other throughput vary by price tier.
- **Pro plan**: All features with top priority generation and benefits.

## Topping up tokens

When you're running low on tokens, you can get more in these ways:

- **Invite friends**: Earn reward tokens when invited friends sign up.
- **Upgrade membership plan**: Upgrade your plan to receive tokens.

## Cancel membership

You can cancel your membership at any time. After you cancel, you can keep using the service until the **next billing date**. Cancellation takes effect at the **start of the next billing cycle**.

Once a subscription period has started, that period is not canceled or refunded mid-cycle. Your membership remains active until the next billing date.
