---
title: "Reward Tokens"
---

Reward tokens come in two types: mission rewards and referral rewards.

## Mission rewards

Reward tokens are earned by completing various reward missions. You can earn them in the following ways and convert them for use. Missions are active for a limited time and may change.

- **Attendance reward**: Rewards given for daily check-in.
- **Create series reward**: Rewards given when you create a series.
- **Profile customization reward**: Rewards given when you customize your profile.

## Referral rewards

Referral rewards are tokens you receive when you invite a friend and that friend signs up for Daven AI or purchases a plan. [Learn more](https://daven.ai/settings?tabs=affiliate)
