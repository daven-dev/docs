- You can choose from various output formats such as infographic newspapers, shorts, cartoons, and more.
- You can reflect characters or logos you created in the output. For detailed application, use advanced settings to configure output-related prompts and other options.
- Output must be assigned to a series. Content generated by Automation Agent is automatically categorized into that series.
