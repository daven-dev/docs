---
title: "What is Automation Agent"
---

Automation Agent generates content you want based on real-time or general information that occurred during a specified time period.

- **Real-time information:** Processes data based on live information such as news and market updates to produce results in the format you want. (e.g., daily editorial cartoons, U.S. market news summary newspapers)
- **General information:** Processes data based on general information such as daily English lessons, golf tips, or daily quotes for reflection to produce results in the format you want. (e.g., a daily note of 10 advanced business English expressions, shorts featuring quotes from notable people born today)

Automation Agent automatically submits generation jobs at scheduled times for repetitive tasks.

Examples:

- Generate various workplace episodes with your character every morning at 7:00 AM: webtoon
- Every morning at 7:00 AM, generate the most important world financial news from the past 12 hours: infographic newspaper format
- Generate shorts with quotes from notable people born today every evening at 9:00 PM: serialized as shorts, also uploaded to YouTube
- Generate neighborhood news for where you live every day at noon: web news style
